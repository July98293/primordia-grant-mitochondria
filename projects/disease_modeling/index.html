Project 05ComputationalOngoing

More Diseases and Organelle-Level Modeling

After leukemia, comparisons against microgravity, infarct, obesity, PCOS and other conditions are queued, together with integration into the secondary transfer project. With enough disease datasets, we could start modeling mitochondrial perturbations computationally: similar in concept to a virtual cell, but focused on the organelle.

Queue

  • Microgravity
  • Infarct
  • Obesity
  • PCOS
  • Others to come

Where this is going

Each new disease adds a view of how mitochondrial programs shift across cell types. Combined with the donor and receiver map, this could become a focused model of mitochondrial perturbations, a kind of "virtual mitochondrion".
