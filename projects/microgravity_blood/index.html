Mitochondrial Transfer in Blood after Microgravity

A planned variation of the neutrophil transfer experiment. Blood would first be exposed to simulated microgravity, then used for mitochondrial transfer, to see whether the altered environment changes uptake or activity.

Idea

This is one of the variations of the neutrophil project I would most like to run. The question is whether exposure to microgravity changes how blood cells take up and keep mitochondria.

Status

I am looking for a clinostat. I can't just use one I build myself, because the experiment needs to be reproducible by other labs.

if you have a clinostat I could borrow, please reach out!

Connection to other work

Microgravity is also queued as a dataset in the disease comparison work, so the wet-lab and computational sides can eventually be compared.
