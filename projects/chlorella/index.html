Cross-Species Transfer: Chlorella to Mammalian Cells

A pilot testing whether mitochondria from a photosynthetic alga are recognised and functionally tolerated by mammalian recipient cells. The design distinguishes three outcomes, and all three are informative.

Objective

  • Determine whether mammalian cells take up Chlorella-derived mitochondria.
  • If they do, determine whether the mitochondria are retained or cleared.

Design

  1. Culture Chlorella.
  2. Isolate mitochondria.
  3. Add to mammalian recipient cells.
  4. Score against three outcomes: no uptake, transient uptake, functional tolerance.

Prior expectation

  • Most likely no uptake.
  • Otherwise transient uptake followed by clearance, on the grounds that these mitochondria are not efficient in a mammalian cell.
  • A functional effect would open the broader question of cross-species compatibility and the limits of mitochondrial transplantation.

Status

  • Waiting on the algae.
  • Experiment scheduled for 20 September.
