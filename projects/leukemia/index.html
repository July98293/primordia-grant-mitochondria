Comparison against Disease: Leukemia

I built a pipeline to identify and characterise mitochondrial receiver programs in disease contexts, starting with acute myeloid leukemia (AML). Compared with healthy donors, mitochondrial genes are modestly but consistently up in T and NK cells, down in erythroid cells, and show no reliable signal elsewhere.

Approach

The pipeline reuses the foundation of the secondary transfer project and applies it to disease datasets. Because datasets come from different studies and conditions, much of the work is choosing comparable datasets and building robust batch correction and normalisation.

The same analysis was re-run for 10 cell groups. Not every group shows significant pathway changes.
