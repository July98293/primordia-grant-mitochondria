Project 07ReviewAnalysis done · writing

Review, Impact Analysis and Dose Modeling

A review of mitochondrial transfer that includes what I believe is the first combined ethical, environmental and market assessment of the field. It also proposes a dose-modeling approach and puts together a case for revisiting mitochondrial transfer in IVF.

How it grew

I initially thought this would be a quick win. I ended up rewriting it about five times, because every conversation with experts on the topic changed something. It is much better for it.

the "side quest" of dose modeling turned into its own chapter

Contents

  • Ethical assessment
  • Environmental impact assessment
  • Market assessment
  • Dose-modeling proposal
  • A case for reopening mitochondrial transfer in IVF

The dose-modeling, environmental, ethical and market analyses are in chapters 4 and 5 of the review.

Status

The analysis is complete. The writing needs polishing, and the figures and ordering still need to be finalised.
